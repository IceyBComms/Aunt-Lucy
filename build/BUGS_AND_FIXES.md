## Patterns

Cross-cutting shapes that keep producing separate-looking bugs. Recorded here ONCE rather than re-argued in every row they touch.

### P1 — Irreversible steps with no way back and no warning

**Three open bugs are the same bug wearing different clothes: a one-way door, taken without being told it was one.**

- **#078** — "Make it live" with zero tasks activates for real, and the pre-built suggestion list can never be reached again.
- **#063** — the occasion is written once at gift creation and is not writable by anyone afterwards; a mis-tap can only be undone by editing the database by hand.
- **#071** — a draft support page cannot be re-opened OR deleted; leaving it strands it on the dashboard permanently.

**What they share, and why it is worth naming:** in each case the product lets someone take an action that CANNOT be undone, gives no warning
beforehand, and offers no correction path afterwards. None of them fails loudly. Nothing errors, nothing is lost visibly, and the person
usually does not discover the door has shut until they go looking for the way back — which is exactly the moment they are least able to cope
with it, because they are already depleted. That is the opposite of the relief the product exists to produce.

⚖️ **The rule this suggests, for anything built from here:** before shipping a step that cannot be undone, decide explicitly which of three it
gets — a warning that names the REAL cost (per Kate's #078 ruling: what is lost, not merely what changes), a correction path afterwards, or an
honest reason it needs neither. "Nobody will do that" is not one of the three.

📌 **Do not fix these as three unrelated tickets.** They will produce three different interaction patterns for the same problem, which is how a
product ends up with three different ways of saying "are you sure?". Logged 30 Aug 2026 at Kate's instruction.
